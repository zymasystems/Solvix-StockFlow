# # Solvix StockFlow

A modern cloud-based inventory and stock management system developed by Zyma Systems. Solvix StockFlow helps businesses streamline inventory tracking, stock control, product management, and reporting through an intuitive web-based platform.

## 🌐 Live Environments

- Production: https://solvixrsa.co.za
- Staging: https://staging.solvixrsa.co.za

## 📋 Overview

Solvix StockFlow is designed to provide businesses with a centralized platform for managing inventory operations efficiently. The system enables organizations to monitor stock levels, manage products, track inventory movements, and generate business insights through reporting tools.

## ✨ Features

### Inventory Management
- Product catalog management
- Stock level monitoring
- Inventory tracking
- Product categorization
- Stock adjustment functionality

### Dashboard & Analytics
- Real-time inventory overview
- Key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Inventory statistics
- Business insights and reporting

### User Management
- Secure user authentication
- Role-based access control
- User account management

### Stock Operations
- Stock additions and deductions
- Inventory movement tracking
- Transaction history
- Audit trail functionality

### Reporting
- Inventory reports
- Stock movement reports
- Business performance insights
- Data export capabilities
